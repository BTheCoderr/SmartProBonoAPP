# 🔍 Finding Your Render.com Account

## ✅ **EASIEST METHOD: Sign in with GitHub**

**You don't need to remember your username!**

1. Go to: https://render.com
2. Click **"Sign In"**
3. Click **"Continue with GitHub"**
4. It will automatically find your Render account(s) linked to your GitHub

1. Go to: https://render.com/signin

### **Option 2: Check Your Email**
1. Search your email inbox for: **"Render"**
2. Look for emails from: **support@render.com** or **no-reply@render.com**
3. These emails will show which email address you used

### **Option 3: Check Browser Saved Passwords**
1. If you use Chrome: `chrome://settings/passwords`
2. Search for: **"render.com"**
3. It will show saved usernames/emails
